# Dataset provenance

`trails.geojson` and `shelters.csv` are generated by `scripts/build_dataset.py`
from curated tables embedded in that script.

**Sources and method:**
- Trail topology (junctions and marked-route connectivity) was curated from
  OpenStreetMap data (Geofabrik extracts for Ivano-Frankivsk and Zakarpattia
  oblasts, `route=hiking` relations in the Chornohora area) and cross-checked
  against public Chornohora route descriptions.
- Segment lengths, ascent/descent, and maximum altitudes are approximations
  derived from OSM geometry and SRTM elevation profiles, rounded for the
  curated tables.
- `exposure` (sheltered / mixed / exposed_ridge) and `river_crossings` were
  assigned manually from route descriptions; they encode domain judgment, not
  raw OSM tags.
- Shelter and camp locations come from OSM `tourism=wilderness_hut` /
  `amenity=shelter` nodes plus well-known established camp sites
  (Nesamovyte, Maricheika, Kozmeshchyk).
- `nearest_settlement` per node is the closest OSM `place` node with an
  OpenWeatherMap-resolvable name; it is what the agent passes to the
  OpenWeather MCP `weather` tool.

**Reproduction:** `python scripts/build_dataset.py` regenerates both files
deterministically. The dataset is opened read-only at server startup; no tool
mutates it.

**Known limitations:** segment geometry is simplified to straight lines between
junction coordinates (attributes, not geometry, drive all tool logic); values
are approximations suitable for the planning heuristics, not for navigation.
